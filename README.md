**🍲 Food Rescue Network System**
A web-based platform designed to reduce food wastage by connecting food donors, NGOs, and volunteers. The system enables restaurants, hotels, event organizers, and individuals to donate surplus food, while NGOs and volunteers coordinate collection and distribution to people in need.

**🚀 Features**
User Registration and Login
Food Donation Request Management
Donor Dashboard
NGO Dashboard
Volunteer Coordination
Food Pickup Scheduling
Real-Time Donation Tracking
Donation History Management
Food Availability Notifications
Secure Database Storage
**🎯 Problem Statement**

Large quantities of edible food are wasted daily while many people face food insecurity. The Food Rescue Network System bridges this gap by providing a centralized platform that facilitates efficient food donation and distribution.

**📂 Project Workflow**
Donors register and log in.
Donors submit surplus food details.
NGOs receive available donation notifications.
Volunteers are assigned for food collection.
Food is collected from donors.
Food is distributed to beneficiaries.
The system updates donation status and records transaction history.
**📈 Key Benefits**
Reduces food wastage.
Supports hunger relief initiatives.
Improves coordination between donors and NGOs.
Enables efficient food distribution.
Maintains transparency through tracking and reporting.
